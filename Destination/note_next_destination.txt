Destination Prediction Model: Complete Breakdown
Goal
Predict where a passenger will want to go given:
predict the destination H3 zone given the origin zone.

Origin zone (H3 cell where pickup happens)
Time features (hour, day of week, weekend/weekday)
Context (rush hour, time period)
H3 zone resolution of 7
This is essentially a multi-class classification problem where each destination H3 zone is a class.

Train with XGBoost


What Happens if Destination is Within Origin Zone?
This is actually a real and common scenario in taxi operations:
Real-World Examples:

Short trips within the same H3 zone (res 7):

H3 resolution 7 cells are approximately 5.16 km² or ~2.27 km across
A passenger might take a taxi from one end of the zone to the other (e.g., from one building to another in the same district)
Examples: Hospital to home within same neighborhood, restaurant to hotel in same area
