🚖 What This Model Predicts:
This model predicts the TOTAL DRIVING DISTANCE of a complete taxi trip - not just passenger distance.
Specifically, it predicts:

The actual route distance a taxi drives from pickup point to drop-off point
Real road distance (accounting for turns, traffic routing, detours)
NOT straight-line distance (which would be much shorter)

What it's based on:

Start location (pickup coordinates)
End location (drop-off coordinates)
Time factors (hour, day of week, month)
Trip characteristics (estimated duration, average speed, idle time)
Geographic zones (H3 hexagonal areas for Bangkok)

Example:

Straight-line distance: Bangna to Suvarnabhumi = 15 km
Model prediction: 22 km (actual driving route with roads, turns, traffic)
Real taxi meter reading: ~23 km

Key Point:
This is the distance that determines:

⛽ Fuel consumption
💰 Taxi fare (distance-based pricing)
⏱️ Driving time (combined with speed)
🛣️ Route planning for your recommendation system

So yes - this predicts the actual passenger trip distance that would appear on the taxi meter, not just geographic distance between two points.
This is exactly what you need for calculating taxi fares and revenue in your route recommendation system! 🎯
